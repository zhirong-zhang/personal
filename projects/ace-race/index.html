Overview

Ace Race is a Multilateral Competition racing board game using playing cards, where players bet on the ace card they think will reach the finish line first. The game is inspired by a game called Horse Race.

Experience Goal:
Players will feel a sense of anticipation and excitement as they watch the Aces race each other and win chips.
